WenQuanYi Micro Hei (文泉驿微米黑) 0.2.0-beta
=============================================

This font is shipped with Model 3-Vulkan so that the interface does not
depend on the host system having a CJK-capable font installed. It is used as
the settings-window and in-game-panel typeface.

Upstream:  https://sourceforge.net/projects/wqy/files/wqy-microhei/
Package:   wqy-microhei.ttc (first face of the collection)
